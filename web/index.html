a door game for Claude Code

A daily-turn text RPG in the spirit of Legend of the Red Dragon. Ten forest fights a day. Three player fights. Gold in your pocket is lost when you die; gold in the bank is safe. Sleep at the inn and nobody can touch you. Sleep in the fields and anyone can. Every morning, one line of news tells you who killed whom.

Log in

In Claude Code, three commands:

❯/plugin marketplace add ericalexander/lord
❯/plugin install dragonmoor@lord
❯/dragonmoor:lord

The first run asks for a name and a class — warrior hits hardest, thief runs away best, mage throws six fireballs a day — and keeps your key on your machine. That's your character; there's no password. Every session after that opens with the realm news.

The day

Forest. Ten fights. Each one is a monster near your level, round by round: attack, special, or run. Win and you keep what it was carrying.

Train. When you've seen enough, your master will test you. You can't run from him.

Challenge. Three fights a day against other players. They're not there to defend themselves, so it's over in one go.

Bank. Five transactions a day. Gold in the bank survives everything.

Inn. Costs a hundred gold a level. Nobody can attack you overnight and you wake up healed.

Fields. Where you sleep when you don't pay. Anyone can find you there and take every coin you're holding.

Reset. Once a day, 1am Eastern, for the whole realm. The dead come back. The dragon, if slain, returns.
